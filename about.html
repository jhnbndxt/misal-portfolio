ABOUT ME

Who is John Creatives?

John Creatives is the artistic identity of John Benidict Misal, a 19-year-old BSIT student at Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Valenzuela. His journey into the world of technology began at just 10 years old, sparked by the inspiration of his uncle’s work with PCs. By 12, he had already discovered a passion for video editing, which soon blossomed into expertise in UI design, system development, and creative media. Now in his second year, he continues to push the boundaries of innovation, blending technology with artistry to bring ideas to life.
